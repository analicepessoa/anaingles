MID-TERM ORAL EXAM A · Intermediate 1

Guia para a Prova Oral — Tell a story about an important moment

Um passo a passo para organizar uma experiência do passado e falar com mais confiança.

🎤 Share with the teacher one of the following moments. Escolha apenas UM tema e conte a história com começo, meio e fim.
1

Choose your topic Escolha o momento sobre o qual você consegue dar mais detalhes

📚
My first day at the English course and how I felt.Meu primeiro dia no curso de inglês e como eu me senti.
🚗
The first time I drove a car.A primeira vez que dirigi um carro.
💼
When I got my first job.Quando consegui meu primeiro emprego.
🏖️
My best vacation.Minhas melhores férias.
👶
When my child was born.Quando meu filho ou minha filha nasceu.
🍳
The first time I cooked a meal.A primeira vez que preparei uma refeição.
Dica: escolha um tema que realmente aconteceu com você e sobre o qual você lembra de detalhes. Você não precisa usar todos os temas.
2

Organize the story before speaking Responda mentalmente às perguntas abaixo para montar sua narrativa

WHEN?Quando aconteceu? Quantos anos você tinha?
WHERE?Onde você estava?
WHO?Quem estava com você?
WHAT?O que aconteceu primeiro, depois e no final?
HOW DID YOU FEEL?Como você se sentiu?
WHY WAS IT IMPORTANT?Por que esse momento foi marcante?
Dica: anote apenas palavras-chave. Na prova, conte a história naturalmente em vez de tentar decorar cada palavra.
3

Use the Simple Past A experiência já aconteceu, então os verbos principais ficam no passado

PresentePassadoExemplo
am / iswasI was nervous.
arewereWe were excited.
gowentI went to the school.
havehadI had a great experience.
feelfeltI felt very happy.
getgotI got my first job.
drivedroveI drove slowly and carefully.
cookcookedI cooked dinner for my family.
meetmetI met new people.
enjoyenjoyedI enjoyed every moment.
Atenção: depois de did ou didn't, use o verbo no presente: “I didn't know”, e não “I didn't knew”.
4

Connect the events and describe your feelings Use conectores para não falar frases soltas

Conectores para organizar a história:

First At the beginning Then After that Later Finally In the end Because But So

Sentimentos que você pode usar:

happy excited nervous afraid surprised proud tired grateful confident relieved
  • At first, I felt nervous, but after a few minutes I felt more confident.
  • I was very excited because it was an important moment for me.
  • In the end, I felt proud and happy.
5

Short models for each topic Use os modelos como referência e troque os detalhes pela sua própria história

First day at the English course
“My first day at the English course was last year. I was nervous because I didn't know my classmates. First, the teacher introduced herself. Then, we did a speaking activity. In the end, I felt happy and more confident.”
First time driving a car
“The first time I drove a car, I was with an adult instructor. At first, I felt nervous, so I drove slowly and carefully. After a few minutes, I felt calmer. It was an important experience because I learned something new.”
First job
“I got my first job a few years ago. I worked with friendly people and learned many new tasks. On my first day, I felt nervous but excited. That job was important because it helped me become more responsible.”
Best vacation
“My best vacation was a trip with my family. We went to a beautiful place and stayed there for a few days. We visited interesting places, took photos and relaxed. I felt very happy because we spent time together.”
When my child was born
“When my child was born, I was very emotional and excited. My family was with me during that special time. I felt grateful and happy. It was one of the most important moments of my life.”
First time cooking a meal
“The first time I cooked a meal, I prepared food for my family. First, I organized the ingredients. Then, I cooked everything carefully. The meal wasn't perfect, but my family enjoyed it. In the end, I felt proud.”
Importante: não decore uma história que não é sua. Use a estrutura do modelo, mas conte seus próprios detalhes.
6

Build your complete answer Siga esta ordem para não se perder durante a prova

  • 1. Introdução: diga qual foi o momento e quando aconteceu.
  • 2. Contexto: explique onde você estava e quem estava com você.
  • 3. Acontecimentos: conte o que aconteceu usando conectores.
  • 4. Sentimentos: explique como você se sentiu antes, durante ou depois.
  • 5. Conclusão: diga por que esse momento foi importante.
Modelo para completar:

“I would like to talk about ____________. It happened ____________. I was with ____________ at/in ____________. First, ____________. Then, ____________. After that, ____________. I felt ____________ because ____________. In the end, ____________. This moment was important to me because ____________.”
Plano B se esquecer uma palavra: continue com uma palavra mais simples. O objetivo é comunicar a história com clareza.
