Complete a história com o passado simples dos verbos entre parênteses.

My weekend really a disaster! On Saturday morning, I late and I the bus to the gym. So I to walk instead. On the way, it to rain, and I an umbrella. When I finally home, I I my keys inside the gym locker!
Ver gabarito
a) was   b) woke up   c) missed   d) decided
e) started   f) didn't bring   g) got   h) realized   i) had left / left
💡 Dica: verbos regulares terminam em -ed (started, decided, realized). Verbos irregulares mudam de forma (be→was, wake up→woke up, get→got, leave→left). Na frase negativa, usamos didn't + verbo no infinitivo (didn't bring, não "didn't brought").

a) I dinner when the phone .

Ver gabarito
a) was cooking / rang
b) was studying / was watching
c) were walking / started

💡 Dica: a ação mais longa (o "cenário") fica no Past Continuous; a ação curta que interrompe fica no Simple Past. Quando as duas ações acontecem ao mesmo tempo (frase b), as duas podem ficar no Past Continuous.
4

Used to (hábitos do passado)

Escreva 3 frases sobre hábitos que você tinha na infância/adolescência e não tem mais, usando used to ou didn't use to.

Ver exemplos
- I used to ride my bike every afternoon.
- I didn't use to like vegetables.
- We used to spend summers at my grandparents' house.
💡 Dica: "used to" não muda com pronome (I/she/they used to...). Na negativa e na pergunta usamos "use to" (sem -d): Did you use to...? / I didn't use to...
5

Modais para dar conselhos

Dê um conselho para cada pessoa, usando modais (should, shouldn't, could, ought to, had better, etc.)

Diana – I've been offered a great job in another city, but my whole family lives here. I don't know if I should move.
Marcus – My neighbor plays loud music every night and I can't sleep. I'm getting really tired, but I don't want to start a fight.
Julia – I found out my best friend has been talking behind my back. Should I confront her about it?
Ver exemplos de resposta
Diana: You should make a list of pros and cons before deciding. You could also talk to your family about it.
Marcus: You ought to talk to him calmly first. You'd better not ignore it, or it will keep happening.
Julia: You should talk to her directly and explain how you feel. You shouldn't assume anything before hearing her side.
💡 Dica: vocabulário útil para dar conselhos: "you should / shouldn't", "you could", "it would be a good idea to...", "you'd better", "why don't you...?", "have you thought about...?"
