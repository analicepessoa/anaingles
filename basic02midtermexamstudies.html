2. Simple Present ou Present Continuous?

Simple PresentPresent Continuous
Usorotina, hábito, fatoagora, ou algo temporário
Palavrasevery day, always, usuallynow, at the moment, this month, temporarily
FormaI work · he worksI am working · he is working
NegativaI do not / he does not workI am not · he is not working
Verbos de estado quase nunca ficam no continuous: be, want, need, think (opinião), know, like. Ex.: "I want water", não "I am wanting".
"get / become" mostram mudança em andamento, então ficam no continuous: I am getting tired.

Exercício: complete com a forma correta

Lucas (be – negative) a patient student, to be honest. His teacher says he (need) to study more, and now he (try) a new method. Every morning he (read) for 30 minutes. At the moment he (prepare) for an important test. He (think) that he (get) better at English. His sister (help) him this month, and she (explain) the grammar every night. But Lucas (want) to be independent, so he (learn) to study alone.

Respostas
is not (isn't) · needs · is trying · reads · is preparing · thinks · is getting · is helping · explains · wants · is learning
Why? every morning/every night = rotina; at the moment/this month = temporário; needs, thinks, wants = verbos de estado.

Modelo (outro lugar)

The nearest bank is two blocks from here. Go straight on Main Street, turn left at the corner, and it is next to the post office.
O que o professor corrige: Grammar (preposições de lugar, frases completas, verbos) e Lexis (expressões de direção e lugares da cidade).

Pratique (escreva e depois compare com o modelo)

a) The nearest bakery.

b) The nearest bus station.

c) The nearest library.

d) The nearest bank.

Exemplo para (b)
The nearest bus station is three blocks from here. Turn right on this street, go straight and it is across from the bakery.

Comece escrevendo onde você está (a escola, sua casa), assim fica mais fácil.

4. Writing: planos para as férias

Responda as 3 perguntas em um texto curto: Where? Transportation? Activities?

Planos futuros: I'm going to + verbo ou I'm + verbo-ing. Ex.: I'm going to visit… / I'm visiting… / I'm taking a bus.

Modelo

Next vacation, I'm going to Gramado with my family. We're taking a bus because it is cheaper than a plane. In Gramado, we're visiting a chocolate museum, walking in the park and eating pizza. I'm very excited!

Sua vez

Erros comuns: "I going" (faltou am) · "I'm go to" (correto: I'm going to) · "in bus" (correto: by bus ou taking a bus) · texto muito curto. Escreva pelo menos 4 frases, com início e fim.

6. Listening: anúncio de transporte

Ouça o anúncio três vezes. Depois marque T (true) ou F (false).

The train number is 5120.
The train's destination is Detroit.
The train's status is canceled.
There is a problem on the tracks.
The train is being replaced.
Boarding is expected to start in five minutes.
The boarding platform is 3.

Respostas
T · F · F · T · F · F · T
Dica: leia as frases ANTES de ouvir e sublinhe números, lugares e tempo. Esses são os detalhes que mudam.
Números: 15 = fifteen (fif-tín, termina forte) · 50 = fifty (fif-ti). Fique atento a -teen e -ty!
