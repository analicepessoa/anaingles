1. Conectores de adição e contraste

Um conector junta duas frases em uma e mostra a relação entre elas. Antes de escrever, sempre pergunte: as duas ideias se somam ou uma vai contra a outra?

TipoConectoresQuando usar
Adiçãoand, also, too, besides, moreover, in addition, as well as, both … and, not only … but alsoAs ideias caminham juntas (duas qualidades, dois fatos que reforçam um ao outro).
Contrastebut, yet, however, nevertheless, nonetheless, although, even though, though, despite, in spite of, despite the fact that, while, whereasUma ideia contraria a expectativa ou se opõe à outra.

Posição e pontuação de cada um

ConectorComo usarExemplo
but, yetno meio, vírgula antesIt was late, but we kept working.
however, nevertheless, nonethelesscomeça nova frase (ou depois de ponto e vírgula), vírgula depoisIt was late. However, we kept working.
although, even though, though+ frase completa (sujeito + verbo)Although it was late, we kept working.
despite, in spite of+ substantivo ou verbo-ingDespite the late hour, we kept working.
despite the fact that+ frase completaDespite the fact that it was late, we kept working.
moreover, besides, in additioncomeça nova frase, vírgula depoisHe is smart. Moreover, he is kind.
alsoantes do verbo principal (ou depois de "be")He also plays tennis.
both … andjunta dois itens do mesmo tipoI like both tea and coffee.
not only … but alsomostra duas coisas, com ênfaseShe not only sings but also dances.

Quando usar e quando NÃO usar

✅ Isabel is tired. She also has a headache. (as duas ideias se somam, então adição)
❌ Isabel is tired. However, she has a headache. (contraste não faz sentido, pois uma não vai contra a outra)
✅ It was raining, but we walked. (a chuva era razão para não caminhar, então contraste)
❌ Although it was raining, but we walked. (nunca use dois conectores de contraste juntos)
❌ Despite it was raining, we walked. → ✅ Despite the rain, we walked. / Despite the fact that it was raining, we walked.
❌ He is rich. Moreover, he is unhappy. (aqui há contraste, então use "but" ou "however")
Resumo rápido: 1) mesma direção → adição · 2) direção oposta → contraste · 3) olhe o que vem depois: frase completa (although) ou só substantivo/-ing (despite) · 4) não coloque dois conectores para a mesma relação. Na prova, várias respostas são aceitas, desde que fiquem corretas e coerentes.

Passo 1: de que tipo é a relação? (Adição ou contraste?)

a) My sister cooks well. She is an excellent baker.

b) He studied a lot. He didn't pass the exam.

c) The film was long. It was very boring.

Respostas
a) adição · b) contraste (esperava-se passar) · c) adição (os dois são pontos negativos, na mesma direção)

Passo 2: junte as frases com um conector adequado

a) Mia studied all night. She failed the test.

b) We stayed at home. It was a sunny day.

c) My brother plays the guitar. He sings very well.

d) I like pizza. I like pasta.

e) The hotel is expensive. It is far from the beach.

f) I'd like to help you. I'm very busy today.

g) Lisbon is beautiful. It is full of tourists.

h) His essay wasn't well organized. He got a good grade.

Respostas (há mais de uma possível)
a) Although Mia studied all night, she failed the test. / …; however, she failed.
b) Even though it was a sunny day, we stayed at home.
c) My brother plays the guitar and also sings very well.
d) I like both pizza and pasta.
e) The hotel is expensive. Besides, it is far from the beach.
f) I'd like to help you, but I'm very busy today.
g) Lisbon is beautiful; however, it is full of tourists. / Despite being beautiful, Lisbon is full of tourists.
h) His essay wasn't well organized. Nonetheless, he got a good grade. / Despite the fact that his essay wasn't well organized, he got a good grade.

Passo 3: corrija o erro

1) Although he is rich, but he is not happy. · 2) Despite she was ill, she went to work. · 3) She is polite. However, she is also kind.

Respostas
1) Although he is rich, he is not happy. (tire o "but")
2) Despite being ill / Despite her illness / Although she was ill, she went to work.
3) She is polite. Moreover (or: In addition), she is kind. ("however" não combina com adição)

2. Calendário: eventos futuros

Na prova você olha o calendário, vê o que está escrito em cada dia e escreve uma frase sobre cada compromisso. Veja o passo a passo:

Passo a passo: 1) ache o evento na caixinha · 2) veja o dia da semana (a coluna) e o número do dia · 3) escreva On + [mês] + [dia] · 4) use a pessoa certa: he/she + verbo com -s.
Modelo da prova: On February 3rd, Jason has a doctor's appointment.
Para planos e agenda, você pode usarExemplo
Simple Present (horário marcado, agenda)On March 4th, Anna has a dentist appointment. · She works until 6 p.m.
Present Continuous (plano combinado)She is having lunch with Carla on Tuesday.
be going to (plano, intenção)She is going to travel to Recife on March 28th.
Cuidado: he/she/it pede -s (has, takes, works, visits, travels) · "on" antes de datas · ordinais: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 12th, 13th, 21st, 22nd, 23rd, 30th · Não basta o nome do evento: faça uma frase completa.

3. Writing: dando sua opinião

Escolha um tema (na prova são 4) e escreva o que pensa, com motivos. O professor avalia modal verbs, conectores, expressões de opinião e se o texto está completo.

Passo a passo

1) Diga a sua opinião · 2) dê dois motivos · 3) mostre o outro lado e responda · 4) conclua.

ParaUse
OpiniãoIn my opinion, … · I think that … · As far as I'm concerned, …
ConcordarI (totally) agree. · I'm in favor of … · That's true.
DiscordarI disagree. · I'm against … · I don't think that's right.
Obrigação / proibiçãomust (be) · should (be) · need to · mustn't (proibido)
OrganizarFirst, … · Besides, … · However, … · Finally, …
Modal verb vem sempre com verbo simples: should be banned ✓, should to ban ✗, musts ✗. Passiva: must be + particípio (Cars must be banned).

Modelo (outro tema)

In my opinion, fast food ads should be banned from children's TV. First, kids believe everything they see, so they ask for unhealthy food. Besides, many families spend too much on it. However, some people say that ads create jobs. Although that is true, health is more important. That's why I agree with this idea.

Sua vez: escolha um

• Uniforms must be worn in all schools. • Smartphones should be banned from classrooms. • Public transport must be free for everyone.

Erros comuns: "I am agree" ✗ → "I agree" ✓ · frase sem verbo · esquecer o motivo · texto curto demais.

4. Writing: texto sobre o futuro

O título pede previsões. Quando usar cada forma de futuro:

FormaQuandoExemplo
will / won'tprevisão, opinião ("I think…")I think cities will be cleaner.
may / mightpossibilidade (talvez)Traffic might still be a problem.
be going toprevisão com evidência, planosLook at those clouds. It is going to rain.
Present Continuouscompromisso já combinadoI am traveling tomorrow.
Time expressions: in the next fifty years · in 2075 · by 2050 · in the future · in a few decades · someday · soon. Vocabulário: technology, pollution, traffic, jobs, robots, health, education, climate, energy.
Regras: will + verbo simples (não "will goes"), título no topo, 6 a 8 frases, conecte as ideias.

Modelo

My City in the Next Thirty Years
I think my city will be very different in thirty years. There will be more electric cars, so the air will be cleaner. People won't use paper money, and robots will help in hospitals. However, the traffic might still be a problem. I hope we will have more parks. I'm sure my city will be a better place to live.

Sua vez: "My School in the Next Twenty Years"

5. Listening: responder com frases

Você ouve um diálogo (duas vezes) e responde cada tópico com uma frase completa, como na prova: "They're staying at …", "The plane leaves at …".

Passo a passo: 1) antes: leia os 5 tópicos e pense no que vai ouvir · 2) 1ª vez: anote palavras-chave, números e horários · 3) 2ª vez: complete o que faltou · 4) depois: transforme as notas em frase (He/She/They + verbo). Se perder algo, siga para o próximo tópico.
Horas: 6:45 = "a quarter to seven" · 8:00 = "eight o'clock". Números: 13 (thirteen) ≠ 30 (thirty).

Responda com uma frase:

Weather forecast

Warm clothes

Place to stay

Activities

Departure time

Respostas e texto do diálogo
It's going to be cold and windy, around twelve degrees. · They need warm jackets and scarves. · They're staying at a small hostel near the park. · They're going hiking on Saturday and visiting a local market on Sunday. · The bus leaves at 6:45 a.m.

Sara: Tom, have you checked the weather for Curitiba?
Tom: Yes. It's going to be cold and windy, around twelve degrees.
Sara: Then we need warm jackets and scarves.
Tom: Right. I booked a small hostel near the park.
Sara: Great! What are we doing there?
Tom: We're going hiking on Saturday, and on Sunday we're visiting a local market.
Sara: And what time is our bus?
Tom: It leaves at a quarter to seven in the morning.
